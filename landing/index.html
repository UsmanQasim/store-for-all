Questions, answered

Is Dukaan really free?

Yes. Customers and shop owners use Dukaan for free. We don’t take a cut of your sales and there are no ads in the app.

Does Dukaan handle my money?

No. Dukaan only shows the shop’s own payment details (JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast, bank transfer, UPI…). You pay from your own wallet or bank app, directly to the shop. Always check the account title matches the shop.

What if the customer doesn’t have the app?

Scanning the shop’s QR with any camera opens a web page with the shop’s payment details and a link to install the app. Once installed, the same QR opens the shop inside the app.

How do bookings work if my shop has more than one chair?

Set how many seats you have and how long a slot is. Dukaan only offers times that still have a free seat, so you never get double-booked.

Can I register more than one shop?

Yes — one account can manage up to five shops and switch between them from the Business dashboard.

How do I delete my account?

In the app go to Profile → Delete account. It’s instant and removes your profile, bookings, orders, reviews and shops. You can also request deletion on the web.
