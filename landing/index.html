Frequently Asked Questions

All your questions about 0% commission, direct payments and your counter QR poster, answered.

How is Dukaan genuinely 0% commission forever?

expand_more
Dukaan is not a payment middleman or delivery aggregator. Customers pay your own JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast or bank account directly, so there is nothing for us to take a cut from. Listing your shop, bookings, pre-orders and your QR poster are free.

Does Dukaan hold or keep customer money in escrow?

expand_more
Never. When a customer pays, money moves from their banking or wallet app straight into the account you added. Dukaan never holds, holds back or delays your funds — we never see the money at all.

How do I get my counter QR poster?

expand_more
Register your shop in the app, add your payment methods and open QR poster. Tap Download A4 Print PDF and print it at any photocopy shop — or share it on WhatsApp. You can also put it in an acrylic stand from your local market.

Do my walk-in customers need to download the app to pay or book?

expand_more
Not to pay. Any smartphone camera can scan your QR — it opens your shop page in the browser with your payment details and a copy button. To book a seat or pre-order, customers use the free Dukaan app, which the same QR helps them install.
