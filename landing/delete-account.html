Delete your Dukaan account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is permanent.

Option 1 — in the app (instant)

  1. Open Dukaan and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Option 2 — without the app

Email us from the address you sign in with. We’ll verify ownership and delete the account within 7 days.

Request deletion by email

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email, phone, photo) and sign-in credentials
  • Your bookings, orders, reviews, favorites, reports and notifications
  • Any shops you own, with their catalog, payment details, offers, bookings and orders
  • Photos you uploaded and your push-notification tokens

What may be kept

Server security logs (IP address, timestamps) for up to 90 days, and encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which they are overwritten. We may keep limited records where the law requires it.
