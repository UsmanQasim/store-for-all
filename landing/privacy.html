Privacy policy

Effective: 24 September 2026 · Applies to the Dukaan Android app and dukaan.app

Dukaan (“we”, “us”) is operated by [Company legal name], [address]. Dukaan helps customers find local shops, book seats, pre-order products, leave reviews and view a shop’s payment details, and helps shop owners manage those requests. This policy explains what data we handle and why. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

2. Payments

Dukaan does not process payments and never receives your money or your wallet/bank credentials. Payment details shown in the app are published by the shop owner. When you pay, you do so in your own wallet or bank app, directly to the shop.

3. Who we share data with

  • Shops you interact with — your name, phone (if given), booking/order details and notes.
  • The public — reviews (with your name and photo) and shop listings.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf under contract: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage — hosted in [region]), Expo (push notification delivery), Google (Play distribution, maps links you open), and our email provider for one-time codes.
  • Legal — when required by law or to protect users’ safety.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Access is restricted by database row-level security so users can only read data they are entitled to.

4. Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account) or at dukaan.app/delete-account. Deletion immediately and permanently removes your profile, bookings, orders, reviews, favorites, notifications, uploaded photos and any shops you own. Backups roll off within 30 days. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.

5. Your choices & rights

  • Edit your profile any time; turn off “Offers from shops I favorite”; disable notifications, location or camera in Android settings.
  • Request a copy of your data or correction by emailing support@dukaan.app. We respond within 30 days.

6. Children

Dukaan is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Changes

We’ll post updates here and notify you in the app for material changes.
