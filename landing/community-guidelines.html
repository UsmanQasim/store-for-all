Community guidelines

Dukaan works when customers and shopkeepers can trust each other. These rules apply to reviews, shop listings, photos, offers and messages.

Do

  • Review shops honestly, based on your own real experience.
  • Keep shop details, prices and payment accounts accurate and up to date.
  • Reply to reviews respectfully.

Don’t

  • Post hate speech, harassment, threats, sexual content or graphic violence.
  • Post fake reviews, review your own shop, or pay/pressure people for reviews.
  • Share other people’s personal information.
  • List illegal or restricted goods and services (weapons, drugs, counterfeit goods, gambling, adult services).
  • Scam customers or publish payment accounts that don’t belong to your shop.

Reporting & blocking

Tap ⋮ → Report on any review, or the flag icon on any shop. You can also Block a user to hide their reviews. Reviews reported by several people are hidden automatically while our team investigates. We review reports within 48 hours and may remove content, restrict or ban accounts.

Contact

Urgent safety issue? Email support@dukaan.app with “Safety” in the subject.
