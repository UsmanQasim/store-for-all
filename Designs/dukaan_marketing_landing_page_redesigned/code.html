Frequently Asked Questions

All your questions regarding 0% commission, Raast payments, and shop standees answered.

How is Dukaan genuinely 0% commission forever?

expand_more
Dukaan does not act as a payment middleman or traditional marketplace aggregator. By leveraging Pakistan's national Raast instant payment rail and direct P2P mobile wallets, transactions bypass heavy interchange fees. Our mission is building open digital infrastructure for the informal economy, funded via optional premium merchant tooling and physical branded standee supplies.

Does Dukaan hold or keep customer money in escrow?

expand_more
Never. When a customer scans the QR code at your shop, money moves directly from their banking or wallet app straight into your registered JazzCash, Easypaisa, or 1Link bank account. Dukaan never holds, holds back, or delays your funds.

How do I get the official acrylic counter standee?

expand_more
Once you complete the 2-minute registration in the app, you instantly receive a printable high-resolution PDF and WhatsApp file. If you want the physical clear-acrylic counter standee delivered to your shop doorstep, request it with one tap from the Merchant Dashboard.

Do my walk-in customers need to download the app to pay or book?

expand_more
No! Any customer with a regular smartphone camera can scan your QR code. It opens a lightweight web interface that shows your catalog, lets them book an appointment slot, or copies your Raast/JazzCash ID in one tap. The native app is optional for power users who want order tracking and push notifications.
